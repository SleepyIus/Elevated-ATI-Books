Sick-Day Rules

What sick-day teaching helps prevent DKA when a client with diabetes is ill?

Content - Ch 83

DKA vs HHS Pattern

Unit 12Ch 83CONTENT

What quick pattern separates DKA from HHS?

DKA has hyperglycemia with ketones and metabolic acidosis. HHS has more profound hyperglycemia with hyperosmolar dehydration and absent or minimal ketones.

Source anchor: Ch 83 DKA and HHS comparison.

Judgment - Ch 83

Potassium and Dextrose Timing

Unit 12Ch 83JUDGMENT

Why are potassium and dextrose timing watched closely during DKA treatment?

Insulin and acidosis correction can shift potassium into cells, so potassium must be monitored and replaced as prescribed. Dextrose is added as glucose approaches 250 mg/dL so insulin therapy can continue while reducing hypoglycemia and rapid osmolar-shift risk.

Source anchor: Ch 83 potassium, acidosis, fluids, and insulin.

Stable Angina vs MI

What feature helps distinguish stable angina from myocardial infarction?

Furosemide and Digoxin Link

Unit 4Ch 32JUDGMENT

Why does a client taking furosemide and digoxin need potassium and toxicity teaching?

Furosemide can cause hypokalemia, which increases digoxin-toxicity risk. Teach the client to report muscle weakness, fatigue, confusion, poor appetite, or other toxicity cues and to follow prescribed pulse parameters.

Why might heparin be prescribed for a client who has DIC?

Heparin can be used to decrease microclot formation, which helps slow consumption of clotting factors in DIC while the underlying cause and bleeding risk are managed.
