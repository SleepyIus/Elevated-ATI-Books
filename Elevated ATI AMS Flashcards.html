Asthma Rescue vs Control

Unit 3Ch 21CONTENT

How do albuterol-type rescue therapy and corticosteroid control therapy differ in asthma?

Short-acting beta2 agonists such as albuterol provide rapid relief of acute manifestations. Corticosteroids and other anti-inflammatory agents decrease airway inflammation for prevention/control rather than immediate rescue.
